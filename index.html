Tips for the best result

Use a photo taken against a plain light wall for the cleanest white-background blend.

Your face should fill 70–80% of the frame — position eyes roughly in the upper-middle third.

Use natural lighting, avoid harsh shadows or coloured ambient light on your face.

Keep a neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open and clearly visible.

Remove glasses — most countries now require glasses-free passport photos.

Check official government requirements for your country before printing.
